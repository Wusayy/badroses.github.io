Field notes

Before you ride

Extra information.

  • Spirit follows a weekly schedule and appears at certain times in certain places. This may vary depending on the day of the week.
  • The schedule goes after the server's official time, which can be observed at the clock tower in Silverglade Village.
  • After 8 pm (20:00) server time, Spirit disappears from the game and reappears the next morning.

Source reference: starstable.wiki.gg · Schedule supplied by Bad Roses
